WHY ONGOING MANAGEMENT?

Your Business Changes. Your Google Profile Should Keep Up.

Setting up your Google Business Profile is a great first step, but it's not always something you can simply set and forget.

Services change, new photos become available, customers leave reviews and business details need updating.

Ongoing management helps keep your profile accurate, useful and professionally presented, while giving you more time to focus on running your business.

Who Is This Service For?

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need ongoing management after setup?

No. You can manage your Google Business Profile yourself. Our ongoing service is for business owners who would prefer professional assistance.

Can you manage an existing Google Business Profile?

Yes. We can review an existing profile and recommend suitable improvements before starting ongoing management.

Do I have to give you my Google password?

No. You can add GJP Digital as a manager through your Google Business Profile. You should never need to share your password.

Will ongoing management guarantee more enquiries?

No. We focus on improving the accuracy, completeness and usefulness of your profile. Google rankings and customer enquiries depend on factors beyond our control.

Can I still make changes to my own profile?

Yes. Your business retains control of its profile, and you can continue making changes whenever you need to.
