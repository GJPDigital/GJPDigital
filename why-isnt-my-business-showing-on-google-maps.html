GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE GUIDE

Why Isn't My Business Showing on Google Maps?

You've searched for your business on Google, but you can't seem to find it. Does that mean something is wrong with your listing?

Not necessarily. There are several reasons your business might not appear where you expect it to. Here's what to check before you start making changes.

START HERE

First, Check How You're Searching

There's a difference between searching for your exact business name and searching for a general service.

For example, searching for "ABC Painting Brisbane" is different from searching for "painters Brisbane".

If your business appears when you search its exact name but not when you search a general service, your profile may already be visible. It just might not appear for that particular search.

Google's local results can vary based on factors including relevance, distance and prominence.

Try searching for your exact business name first, then check whether the information displayed is accurate.

COMMON REASONS

Why Your Business Might Not Be Appearing

1. You Haven't Created a Profile

If your business doesn't have a Google Business Profile, you may need to create one before you can manage how your business appears on Google Search and Maps.

Some businesses already have an automatically generated listing, so it's worth searching before creating a new profile.

2. Your Profile Isn't Verified

Google may require you to verify that you're authorised to represent the business.

Verification helps confirm that the profile belongs to a legitimate business. An unverified profile may have limited visibility or management options.

3. Your Profile Is Suspended or Restricted

Google may restrict or suspend profiles that don't meet its guidelines or require further review.

If you see a suspension or restriction notice, review the reason and Google's appeal process before making changes.

4. Your Business Information Needs Attention

Missing, inaccurate or inconsistent information can make it harder for Google and customers to understand your business.

Check your business name, category, services, contact details and service areas.

5. Your Profile Is New or Recently Changed

Newly created profiles and recent edits may take time to be reviewed or reflected across Google's services.

If you've only just completed verification or made changes, check your profile status before assuming something is wrong.

6. Other Businesses Are Ranking Above You

Your profile might be active but not showing prominently for a competitive search.

Google considers factors such as relevance, distance and prominence when displaying local results. No business is guaranteed a particular position.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

A Simple Checklist to Work Through

Before paying for help or creating another listing, work through these checks:

  • Search Google Maps for your exact business name.
  • Check whether an existing listing already represents your business.
  • Sign in to the Google account that manages your Business Profile.
  • Look for verification, suspension or other status notices.
  • Review your primary business category and contact details.
  • Check your address or service-area settings.
  • Allow time for recent changes to be processed.
  • Use Google's official support resources if your profile is restricted.

You can manage your Business Profile through Google's official tools. Start at Google Business Profile .

FOR TRADIES & MOBILE BUSINESSES

What If You Don't Have a Shopfront?

Many tradies, cleaners and property maintenance businesses travel to customers instead of operating from a public shop.

Eligible businesses can use a service-area Google Business Profile without publicly displaying their home address.

If you don't serve customers at your business address, Google's guidelines generally require you to hide that address.

You can still describe the areas your business serves, but adding more suburbs doesn't guarantee you'll appear in searches throughout those areas.

Example: Brisbane Painter

A painter who works from home and visits customers around Brisbane may be eligible for a service-area profile.

Their profile can explain the services they provide and the areas they cover, without publicly displaying their residential address.

Google's eligibility and verification requirements still apply.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for a new business to appear on Google Maps?

There isn't one guaranteed timeframe. Verification, review and processing times can vary. Check your profile status and Google's current guidance for any outstanding steps.

Why can I find my business by name but not by service?

Your profile may be active but not ranking prominently for that particular search. Local results depend on factors including relevance, distance and prominence.

Should I create another Google Business Profile?

Not simply because your existing profile isn't appearing where you expect. Duplicate profiles can cause problems. Check whether your business already has a listing before creating another one.

Do I have to pay Google to appear on Maps?

No. Creating and managing an eligible Google Business Profile is free. You may choose to pay a service provider for assistance, but Google doesn't charge for the Business Profile itself.
