COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a website if I already have Facebook?

Not necessarily, but a website gives your business a dedicated place to organise information, showcase services and provide clear contact options. It can work alongside your Facebook page.

Can I use my existing domain name?

Often, yes. If you already own a domain, we can review how it's currently configured and discuss connecting it to your new website.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. We build websites with responsive layouts so they're designed to adapt to different screen sizes.

Can I add more pages later?

Yes. Depending on the website's setup, additional pages and features can usually be added as your business grows.

Are hosting and domain costs included?

Hosting, domain registration and any ongoing maintenance arrangements will be discussed before work begins. The exact costs depend on your website's requirements.

Will my website appear at the top of Google?

No specific Google ranking can be guaranteed. We can build your website with basic SEO foundations, but search visibility depends on several factors and may require additional ongoing work.
