Common Questions From Small-Business Owners

Is creating a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Google doesn't charge businesses to create or manage a Google Business Profile. You can set one up yourself if your business meets Google's eligibility requirements.

Services like ours are optional. You're paying for help with setup, optimisation or management, not for the Google listing itself.

Can I have a Google Business Profile without a shop?

Yes, eligible service-area businesses can have a profile even if they don't operate a public shopfront. For example, a tradie who travels to customers may qualify.

Google's eligibility and address-display requirements still apply.

Do I need to pay for Google Ads to appear on Google?

No. Google Ads are paid advertising, while Google Business Profiles and organic website listings can appear without paying for advertising.

However, appearing in a particular position in unpaid search results isn't guaranteed.

Can I ask customers to leave Google reviews?

Yes. Businesses can ask customers for genuine reviews, provided they follow Google's policies.

Requests should be neutral. Avoid offering incentives or only asking customers who you expect to leave positive feedback.

Will having a website automatically bring in customers?

Not automatically. A website gives customers somewhere to learn about your business and enquire, but attracting visitors may also require local SEO, referrals, social media or other marketing activities.
