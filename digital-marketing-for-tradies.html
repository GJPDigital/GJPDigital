COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions We Can Help With

Do I need a website to get my trade business on Google?

No. Eligible trade businesses can have a Google Business Profile without a website. A website can provide additional information for potential customers, but it isn't a requirement for having a Google listing.

Can you help if I work from home and travel to customers?

Yes. Many tradies operate as service-area businesses. Depending on Google's eligibility requirements, you may be able to have a Google Business Profile that shows your service areas without displaying your home address publicly.

What if I already get plenty of work through referrals?

That's a great position to be in. You may not need extensive marketing services. Having accurate information online can still help referred customers check your business and get in touch.

Do I have to sign up for ongoing management?

No. Some services can be completed as one-off projects, while others involve ongoing support. We'll explain the options before you commit.

Do you only work with businesses in Brisbane?

No. GJP Digital is Brisbane-based, but many of our services can be provided remotely to eligible businesses across Australia.
