Think about the last time you searched for a business on Google. Chances are, you looked at its reviews before deciding whether to get in touch.

Google reviews give people an opportunity to hear from real customers who've already used your services.

KEEP IT SIMPLE

It Doesn't Have to Be Awkward

Here's an example of a message a tradie could send after completing a job:

"Hi! Thanks again for choosing us for your project. If you've got a spare minute, we'd really appreciate an honest Google review about your experience. Here's the link: [review link]. Thanks for supporting our small business!"

That's it. No pressure, no complicated sales pitch. Just an easy way for customers to share their experience.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I ask customers directly for Google reviews?

Yes. You can invite customers to share genuine feedback about their experience. Requests should be neutral and should not offer incentives in exchange for reviews.

Does the system automatically send messages?

Not necessarily. Some businesses prefer a simple manual process, while others may benefit from automation. We'll discuss what's practical for your business before recommending a solution.

Do I need lots of customers for this to be useful?

No. Even if you only complete a few jobs each week, having a consistent process can make it easier to invite customers to share their experiences.

Can you guarantee five-star reviews?

No. Customers should always be free to leave honest feedback, whether positive or negative. We don't guarantee ratings or review numbers.

Do I need ongoing management?

Not necessarily. A straightforward review request system may be suitable as a one-off setup, depending on your requirements.
