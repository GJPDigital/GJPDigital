LOCAL SEO EXPLAINED

What Actually Is Local SEO?

Local SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) is about improving how your business appears in search results when people look for services in a particular area.

For example, someone might search "house painter Ipswich", "concreter near me" or "solar panel cleaning Brisbane".

Google uses different signals to decide which businesses and websites to show. Local SEO focuses on making your business information clear, relevant and consistent so search engines can better understand what you offer and where you operate.

Local SEO Is Particularly Useful If...

  • You serve customers in specific suburbs or regions
  • You want more people to discover your services through Google
  • Your business relies heavily on referrals or Facebook
  • You have a website but aren't sure if people can find it
  • You want to build your online presence over time

A REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

Imagine Someone Needs a Concreter in Brisbane

They've decided to get a new driveway and head straight to Google to look for a local concreting business.

They might find a map showing nearby businesses, followed by websites offering concreting services.

If your website clearly explains your concreting services, includes useful information about the areas you work in and is easy to navigate, it gives search engines and customers a better understanding of your business.

That's the kind of foundation local SEO helps you build.

What Might a Customer Search?

  • Concreter Brisbane
  • Driveway concreting near me
  • Concrete slab installation
  • Local concreting contractors
  • Concreting services in North Brisbane

Different searches can show different results. Understanding what your customers look for helps guide your website content.

UNDERSTANDING THE DIFFERENCE

Isn't This the Same as Google Business Profile Management?

Not quite! Your Google Business Profile is one part of your local online presence, but local SEO covers more than just your Google listing.

Google Business Profile management focuses on keeping your profile accurate, useful and updated.

Local SEO can also involve improving your website, its content, how your services are organised and the consistency of your business information online.

The two services can complement each other, but you don't necessarily need both at the same time.

Google Profile vs Local SEO

Google Business Profile Management

Focuses on your Google listing, business information, updates, photos and review support.

Local SEO

Takes a broader look at your website, local search relevance and online business information.

We'll help you understand which areas are worth prioritising.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a website for local SEO?

A website allows for a wider range of local SEO improvements, but eligible businesses can still benefit from optimising their Google Business Profile and maintaining accurate business information online.

How long does local SEO take to work?

Results vary depending on your industry, competition, existing online presence and the improvements being made. SEO is generally a longer-term process rather than an immediate fix.

Can you guarantee I'll rank first on Google?

No. Nobody can guarantee a particular organic or local search ranking. We focus on practical improvements that support your business's visibility and online presence.

Is local SEO only useful for tradies?

Not at all. Local SEO can benefit many types of businesses that serve customers in specific areas, including cleaners, beauty services, property maintenance businesses and other local service providers.

Do I need ongoing SEO services?

Not always. Some businesses benefit from one-off improvements, while others may need ongoing work depending on their goals and local competition.
