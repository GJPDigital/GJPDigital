COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a website to have a Google Business Profile?

No. Eligible businesses can have a Google Business Profile without a website. Having a website can still be useful for giving customers more information.

Can I have a profile if I work at customers' properties?

Yes, many eligible service-area businesses, including trades, can use Google Business Profiles without displaying a home address. Google's eligibility rules still apply.

Will I own my Google Business Profile?

Yes. Your business should retain primary ownership of its profile. GJP Digital can be granted appropriate access to assist with setup and management.

Will my business immediately appear at the top of Google?

No. Google determines local rankings using multiple factors. A complete, accurate profile is a useful starting point, but specific rankings and customer enquiries cannot be guaranteed.

Do I need ongoing monthly management?

No. Setup is available as a standalone service. If you'd prefer help maintaining and improving your profile afterwards, ongoing management is also available.
